### 1. **Meet People Where They Are**

Not where they "should" be.

```
Templated: "Here's the five-year plan..."
```

Your weirdness is data, not flaw.

```
Templated: "That's not how successful people..."
```

### 3. **Small Moves Over Grand Plans**

Perfection kills. Progress heals.

```
Templated: "Here's the complete 10-step strategy..."
Offscript: "What's the next tiny move? What feels true right now?"
```

### 4. **Support, Not Rescue**

```
Templated: "Let me fix this for you..."
```

### 5. **Real Over Normal**

Normal doesn't exist. Real does.

```
Templated: "Act like this so people accept you..."
Offscript: "What if you were completely yourself? What would happen?"
```
